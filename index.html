Filling in the bracket data

The default data of the bracket is the power bracket data for Rustenburg.

If this data is not the same for your region, you can change the values as needed. You can find these values online on your municipality's website.

Payment Amount

It is important to note that the progress through the brackets resets on the first day of the month, starting back at bracket one.

This means that subsequent payments through the month do not start at the first bracket again, but rather up to where the total amount of units purchased for the month left you.

This calculator is currently only able to calculate from the first payment of the month.

If previous payments have been made this month, input the total amount of those payments with the new amount to get the total amount of units.
